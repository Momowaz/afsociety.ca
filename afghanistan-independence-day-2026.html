August 22, 2026 · Holland Park, Surrey, British Columbia

Afghanistan’s 107th Independence Day Celebration

A significant community gathering honouring Afghanistan’s national heritage and celebrating the Afghan community’s cultural roots and home in Canada.

Event Report

Honouring History, Culture, and Community

The Afghan National Cultural Society hosted a commemorative event marking Afghanistan’s 107th Independence Day at Holland Park in Surrey. The celebration brought together a distinguished gathering of elected officials, community leaders, professionals, and representatives from a wide range of public institutions and organizations.

The event was attended by Members of Parliament, MLAs, municipal councillors, community leaders, university executives, health officials, representatives of law-enforcement agencies, members and representatives of the Afghanistan General Council, and other distinguished guests. Their participation reflected the importance of Afghanistan’s Independence Day to the Afghan community and the broader Canadian community.

During the celebration, Hameed Khan Wafa, Chairman of the Afghan National Cultural Society, delivered a speech commemorating Afghanistan’s Independence Day. In his remarks, he spoke about the significance of the occasion and emphasized the importance of preserving Afghan history, culture, traditions, and national identity for future generations.

Chairman Hameed Khan Wafa expressed his sincere appreciation to all distinguished guests, elected representatives, community leaders, organizations, volunteers, and community members who attended and supported the celebration. On behalf of the Afghan community, he thanked all participants for joining the event and for their continued friendship, support, and engagement with the Afghan community in British Columbia, Canada.

The celebration provided an opportunity for Afghan Canadians to come together to honour Afghanistan’s history, national identity, culture, and heritage. It also served as a platform for strengthening relationships between the Afghan community and government officials, civic leaders, educational institutions, health organizations, law-enforcement agencies, and other community partners.

Speakers and distinguished guests recognized the contributions of Afghan Canadians to the social, cultural, economic, and civic life of British Columbia and Canada. The gathering also highlighted the importance of unity, cultural preservation, community engagement, and continued cooperation among Afghan community organizations and Canadian institutions.

The Afghan National Cultural Society’s hosting of the event demonstrated the organization’s continued commitment to preserving Afghan culture and traditions while promoting positive engagement with wider Canadian society.

Overall, the celebration brought people together in a spirit of respect, friendship, cultural pride, and cooperation. The participation of elected representatives, public officials, community leaders, and distinguished guests contributed greatly to the success and significance of the occasion.

The event served as a meaningful celebration of Afghanistan’s national heritage and an expression of the Afghan community’s strong connection to its cultural roots and its home in Canada.
